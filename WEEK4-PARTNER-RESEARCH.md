## TOOLS TO HELP RESEARCH

**Finding emails:**
- Google "[Firm Name] contact" → usually finds contact page
- Try "[firstname]@[domain].com" pattern (e.g., john@lawfirm.com)
- LinkedIn: Search company → click "Contact info" (if public)
- Cold email finder: hunter.io (free tier, finds business emails)

**Finding businesses:**
- Google Maps: Search "[category] [city]" → filter by reviews/rating
- Yelp: Search "Couriers in Nanaimo" → see who else is ranking, then look at their "services" to find B2B partners
- Local directories: Nanaimo Chamber of Commerce (members list online)
- Industry: Industry Canada, Better Business Bureau (see competitors + partners)
