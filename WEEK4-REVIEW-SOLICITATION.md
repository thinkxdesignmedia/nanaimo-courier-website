## WHEN TO SEND EACH

**Email 1 (Google Review Focus):**
- Send 1–2 days after delivery
- Use for all customer types
- Primary goal: Get Google reviews ASAP (most visible)

**Email 2 (Multiple Platforms):**
- Send if no response to Email 1 after 3 days
- Cast wider net (Yelp, Facebook, LinkedIn)
- Good for diverse customer base

**Email 3 (Incentive-Based):**
- Use for repeat customers or high-value orders
- Motivates action (10% discount)
- Track discount codes so you know which customers reviewed

**Email 4 (B2B Professional):**
- Send to medical clinics, law firms, manufacturers
- More formal tone (not casual)
- Emphasizes professionalism

## BULK SENDING TIPS

**Week 4 Goal: Solicit 5–8 reviews**

**Day 5–7 Actions:**
1. Make a list of past customers (create list from order history or memory)
2. Sort by: Recent deliveries (last 1–2 weeks) + High satisfaction (no issues)
3. Send Email 1 (Google focus) to batches of 3–5 per day
4. Track in spreadsheet: Who got emailed, which platform, response status
5. Follow up 3 days later with Email 2 or 3 if no response

**Realistic expectation:** 20–30% response rate = 2–3 reviews from 8 solicitations.
