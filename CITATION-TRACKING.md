## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

**Citations:**
- NAP must be EXACTLY consistent (spaces, hyphens, etc.)
- Use full address on Google Business Profile (this becomes the canonical source)
- Upload 5–10 photos (pickup/delivery, team, office, vehicles)
- Fill out ALL fields on each platform (fuller profiles = higher authority)

**Backlinks:**
- Personalize each outreach email (no mass templates)
- Mention something specific about their business ("I notice you serve law firms in Nanaimo...")
- Offer reciprocal link (shows good faith)
- Follow up 3 days after initial email if no reply
- Thank them when link goes live

**Reviews:**
- Always respond (positive OR negative)
- Keep responses brief (2–3 sentences)
- Offer to fix any issues (for negative reviews)
- Reward reviews with discount code ("Thanks for the review — here's 10% off your next delivery")

**Search Console:**
- Check it EVERY MONDAY
- Log metrics in this sheet (makes trends visible)
- If ranking drops, investigate: Did a new competitor launch? Did your content change? Does a competitor have better backlinks?
