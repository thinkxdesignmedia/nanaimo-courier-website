## PHASE 2: PARTNERSHIP LINKS (Tier 2 — Strategic Authority)

These are reciprocal or one-way links from complementary businesses. **Target: 5–10 by Day 7 of Week 4.**

### Partner Categories (Non-Competing):

**1. Law Firms (Nanaimo + Victoria)**
- Target: 5–8 firms
- Pitch: "We deliver for your clients" → reciprocal link on "Recommended Services" page

**Firms to Reach (Research Locally):**
- Nanaimo Bar Association member directory
- Local law firm websites with "Recommended Partners" pages
- Example: [Search "Nanaimo law firm" + "recommended partners"]

**2. Medical Clinics & Laboratories (Nanaimo + Island)**
- Target: 5–8 clinics/labs
- Pitch: "We deliver for your specimen transport" → link on "Partners" or "Services" page

**Clinics to Reach:**
- RGN Hospital (Comox Valley regional partner)
- Independent medical clinics in Nanaimo
- Diagnostic labs (Lifelabs, other regional labs)

**3. Manufacturing & Industrial (Port Alberni, Nanaimo, Comox)**
- Target: 3–5 companies
- Pitch: "We deliver equipment parts same-day" → link on "Suppliers" page
- Link Format: "Nanaimo Industrial Courier — Equipment parts and urgent freight"

**Companies to Reach:**
- Port Alberni mills/forestry operations

**4. Print & Design Studios**
- Target: 2–3 studios
- Pitch: "Fast proofing and collateral delivery" → "Recommended Services" link

**5. Pharmacy Networks**

## PHASE 3: BACKLINK VERIFICATION & MONITORING

### Tools (Free & Paid):

**Google Search Console (Free)** — REQUIRED
- https://search.google.com/search-console
- Check: "Links" section → see all external sites linking to you
- Verify backlinks being discovered
- Monitor "Top Linking Sites" monthly

**Ahrefs Backlink Checker (Free)** — OPTIONAL
- https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker
- Paste your domain, see all known backlinks
- Monitor domain authority (DA) as it changes

**MozBar Chrome Extension (Free)** — OPTIONAL
- Check page authority (PA) of sites linking to you
- Higher PA = more valuable backlink

### Verification Checklist:

- [ ] Backlink created on partner site
- [ ] Link is visible (not behind login)
- [ ] Link text includes "Nanaimo Courier" or anchor keyword
- [ ] Link points to https://nanaimocourier.com (or relevant page)
- [ ] Link is `<a href="">` (followable, not nofollow)
- [ ] Verify in Google Search Console within 1–2 weeks

## PHASE 4: REVIEW VELOCITY STRATEGY

**Goal:** 20+ 5-star reviews by end of Month 2 (solicit 2–3/week).

### Review Generation Channels:

**1. Google Business Profile (Primary)**
- Post-delivery email: "Leave a review" link
- SMS if available: Short review link
- In-person: Hand out review cards

**2. Ask Existing Customers**
- Email list to past clients (if available)
- Phone follow-up: "How was your delivery? Would you leave a review?"
- In-person at pickup/delivery

**3. Yelp**
- Direct customers to Yelp profile after delivery
- Yelp encourages recent customer reviews

### Review Solicitation Email Template:

```
Subject: How was your Nanaimo Courier delivery?

Hi [Customer],

Thanks for using Nanaimo Courier! We'd love to hear how we did.

If you have a moment, would you mind leaving a quick review? It helps other businesses 
find us and means the world to our team.

⭐ Leave a Google Review: [Google Review Link]
⭐ Leave a Yelp Review: [Yelp Profile Link]

Your feedback drives us to keep improving.

Thanks,
Nanaimo Courier
(250) 753-0000
```

### Target Metrics:

| Month | Target Reviews | Avg Rating | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
