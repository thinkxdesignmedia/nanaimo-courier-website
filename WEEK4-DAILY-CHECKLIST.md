## CONTINGENCY NOTES

**If no replies to outreach:**
- This is normal. 20–30% response rate is good.
- After 3 days, send FOLLOW-UP email.
- Consider: Phone call as last resort (might be more effective for B2B).

**If citations still pending verification:**
- Check spam folder for verification emails
- Resend verification link
- Some citations take 3–5 business days

**If no reviews coming in:**
- Use Email 3 (incentive-based: 10% off)
- Follow up with phone calls to most recent customers ("Did you get my email?")
- Make it easier: Send direct Google review link in text message (faster than email)
